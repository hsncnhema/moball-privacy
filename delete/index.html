Moball League — Delete your account

Developer and data controller: PerseveranceStudio · Contact: moball.league@gmail.com

This page is for players who want their Moball League account, and the data tied to it, deleted. You do not need the game installed to ask.

How to ask for deletion

  1. Find your friend ID. In the game, open FRIENDS: the short code shown there (called "ID") is the name of your account for us. If you can no longer open the game, tell us your nickname and the e-mail address you linked, if any.
  2. E-mail moball.league@gmail.com with the subject Delete my Moball League account and your friend ID. If you linked an e-mail address to the account, write from that address so we can tell it is you.
  3. We delete the account within thirty days.

In the game this page opens from SETTINGS › DELETE ACCOUNT.

What is deleted

Your account and everything attached to it: the sign-in identities (the hash of the secret your phone generated, a linked Google identifier, a linked e-mail address), your nickname, career statistics, friendships, friend requests and invitations. Your name is detached from the match records you appear in; the records themselves (score, date, arena) stay, without your name.

What is kept, and for how long

Encrypted backups of our database are kept so that a server failure does not erase every player's account. They cannot be edited, and they expire on their own: daily copies after about thirty-five days, monthly copies after about four hundred days. A deleted account is removed from the live service when we process your request, and it disappears from the backups as they expire. Voice chat is never recorded, so there is no audio to delete.

Other requests

You can ask us the same way for a copy of the data we hold about you, or to correct it. The full policy, including your rights under the KVKK and the GDPR, is at hsncnhema.github.io/moball-privacy.
