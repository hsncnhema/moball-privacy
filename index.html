Moball League — Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 26, 2026
Developer: PerseveranceStudio · Contact: hasancanbalay@gmail.com

Moball League is a 3v3 online arena football game. This page explains what data the game touches and what it does not.

No accounts, no data collection by us

The game does not require an account or registration. We do not operate our own servers and we do not collect, store, share, or sell personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your nickname, sound settings, and career statistics (matches played, wins, goals) are stored only on your device. They never leave it, and uninstalling the game deletes them.

Online matches (Photon Cloud)

Online multiplayer is powered by Photon Cloud, operated by Exit Games GmbH. When you play an online match, your device connects to Photon's servers: your IP address and connection data are processed transiently to route gameplay traffic between players, and the nickname you chose is visible to other players in the same match. We do not receive or store this data. Photon's own privacy policy is available at photonengine.com/privacy.

No analytics, no ads

The game contains no third-party analytics and no advertising.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included.

Changes

If the game later adds features that change any of the above (for example ads or online accounts), this policy will be updated and the current version will always be available at this address.
