Moball League — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026 (replaces the version of September 10, 2026)
Developer and data controller: PerseveranceStudio · Contact: hasancanbalay@gmail.com

Moball League is a 3v3 online arena football game. This page explains what data the game touches, where it is kept, who can see it, and how to have it deleted. It is written to be read, not skimmed past — if anything here is unclear, write to us.

In short

  • The game creates a player account for you automatically. No e-mail, password or name is required.
  • Linking a Google account or an e-mail address is optional; it only exists so you can get the same account back on a new phone.
  • Results of online matches played on our servers are recorded by the server, for your career statistics.
  • Voice chat is relayed live and never recorded.
  • No advertising, no analytics, no location, no contacts, nothing sold or shared.

Your account

Automatic account. The first time the game starts it generates a random secret, keeps it on your device, and asks our identity service for a player account. The service stores a one-way hash of that secret, a random public player ID, the nickname you type (the last one we saw), when the account was created and last seen, and — once you open the FRIENDS screen — a short friend ID (called "ID" in the game) that you can give to other players. That is the whole account. Uninstalling the game removes the secret from your device; it does not delete the account on our side (see Deleting your data below).

Google sign-in (optional). If you link a Google account, we receive a signed token from Google and keep only the identifier Google assigns to your account for this game. We do not receive or store your Google e-mail address, name or photo. Google's own privacy policy applies to what Google does on its side: policies.google.com/privacy.

E-mail (optional). If you link an e-mail address, we store the address (lower-cased) and, while a sign-in code is pending, a hash of that six-digit code; the code expires after ten minutes or five wrong attempts. The address is used only to send you sign-in codes.

Sessions. The game holds sign-in tokens that let it act as you. The service stores only hashes of them: a short-lived one (one hour) and a longer one (ninety days, renewed while you keep playing). An abandoned device stops being able to sign in after ninety days.

Online matches

Online multiplayer runs on a game server we operate ourselves, with player connections relayed by Photon Cloud (Exit Games GmbH, region: Türkiye). When you play online, your device connects to both: your IP address and connection data are processed transiently to route gameplay traffic, and the nickname you chose is visible to everyone in the same arena. A short-lived ticket (ten minutes) from our identity service tells the game server which account is sitting in your seat; if the identity service cannot be reached, the game still lets you play, anonymously, and that match is not recorded to anyone's career. Photon's privacy policy: photonengine.com/privacy.

What a match record contains. When an online match on our servers ends, the game server — never your phone — records: a match identifier, the arena, the final score, the game version, and for each human player their team, goals, assists, steals, tackles, whether they won, whether they were named player of the match, and which hero they played. These records feed your career statistics and, later, league standings. Practice matches and matches against bots only are never recorded. Records are kept for as long as your account exists.

Friends and presence

Friend requests, friendships and lobby invitations (which expire after two minutes) are stored so the FRIENDS screen can show them. While the game is open it tells the service which arena you are in, if any; this "presence" is what lets your friends see you as online and join you. It expires on its own about a minute after the game closes or loses its connection, and it is shown only to your friends.

Finding players. Any player can search for others by nickname (partial matches, two letters or more) or by their exact friend ID, and open a public profile card showing the nickname, career statistics and whether you are friends. Presence is never shown on that card. There is currently no setting to hide from nickname search; if you need that, write to us and we will handle it by hand until there is.

Voice chat

The game includes optional in-match voice chat. Your microphone is off by default and is only used after you switch it on yourself, from the microphone button in a match or from Settings › Voice Chat. You can switch it off again at any time. Before the microphone opens for the first time, the game asks you to accept a short set of house rules.

While it is on, your voice is streamed in real time through Photon's voice relay (region: Türkiye) to the other players in the same arena and is audible to all of them, not only your team. The audio is never recorded, stored, or sent to us — it exists only for as long as it takes to reach the other players.

Moderation. You can mute any player for yourself, and you can report a player. A report is written to our game server's log with your nickname and player ID, the reported player's nickname and ID, and the reason you picked; we review reports by hand and may restrict or ban accounts on the strength of them. Because voice is never recorded, a report never contains audio.

Where your data is kept

Our game server and identity service run on a rented server in İstanbul, Türkiye. The database is backed up daily on that server; encrypted copies of those backups may also be stored with a cloud storage provider in the European Union, so that a failure of the server does not erase every player's account. Match and voice traffic is relayed by Photon's servers in the Türkiye region. Connections to our services are encrypted (TLS); secrets and tokens are stored only as hashes.

What we, the operators, can see

To run the service and act on reports we can look up an account's nickname, linked e-mail address (if any), which sign-in methods it has, its career statistics, friends, recent matches and current presence. We do not sell, rent or share any of this, and we do not use it for anything other than running the game and handling reports.

What we do not collect

No third-party analytics, no advertising, no location, no contacts, no advertising identifiers, and no device identifiers other than the random secret the game generates itself. Google sign-in gives us no e-mail, name or photo.

Deleting your data, and your rights

There is no in-game button for account deletion yet. To delete your account, e-mail us at hasancanbalay@gmail.com with the friend ID shown on your FRIENDS screen (from the linked e-mail address, if you have one, so that we can tell it is you). We will delete the account, its sign-in identities, career, friendships and invitations within thirty days, and detach your name from the match records you appear in. You can ask us the same way for a copy of the data we hold about you, or to correct it. Players in Türkiye have these rights under the KVKK and players in the European Union under the GDPR; we answer everyone the same way.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. Because voice chat lets players speak to each other directly, parents should be aware of it before letting a younger player use it; it can be left switched off, which is how the game ships.

Changes

If the game later adds features that change any of the above (for example rewarded advertising, leagues, or an in-game way to delete your account), this policy will be updated and the current version will always be available at this address.
