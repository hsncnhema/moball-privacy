Moball League — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026
Developer: PerseveranceStudio · Contact: hasancanbalay@gmail.com

Moball League is a 3v3 online arena football game. This page explains what data the game touches and what it does not.

No accounts, no data collection by us

The game does not require an account or registration. We do not collect, store, share, or sell personal data. We run game servers that pass match traffic between players in real time, but nothing that passes through them is recorded or kept.

Data stored on your device

Your nickname, sound settings, and career statistics (matches played, wins, goals) are stored only on your device. They never leave it, and uninstalling the game deletes them.

Online matches

Online multiplayer is powered by Photon Cloud, operated by Exit Games GmbH, together with a game server we operate ourselves. When you play an online match, your device connects to these servers: your IP address and connection data are processed transiently to route gameplay traffic between players, and the nickname you chose is visible to other players in the same match. None of it is written to disk or retained after the match. Photon's own privacy policy is available at photonengine.com/privacy.

Voice chat

The game includes optional in-match voice chat. Your microphone is off by default and is only used after you switch it on yourself, from the microphone button in a match or from Settings › Voice Chat. You can switch it off again at any time.

While it is on, your voice is streamed in real time to the other players in the same arena and is audible to all of them, not only your team. The audio is relayed live and is never recorded, stored, or sent to us — it exists only for as long as it takes to reach the other players, and nothing remains afterwards. It is not used for any other purpose.

No analytics, no ads

The game contains no third-party analytics and no advertising.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. Because voice chat lets players speak to each other directly, parents should be aware of it before letting a younger player use it; it can be left switched off, which is how the game ships.

Changes

If the game later adds features that change any of the above (for example ads or online accounts), this policy will be updated and the current version will always be available at this address.
